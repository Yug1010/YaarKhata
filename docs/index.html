YaarKhata Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

YaarKhata is a personal finance tracker. All your data stays on your device.

  • Data we collect: None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no network access, and requests no permissions.
  • Data you enter (accounts, balances, expenses, friends' names, debts) is stored only in a local database on your phone.
  • Backups: If you use Export, the app writes a JSON file to a location you choose. Where that file goes afterwards is up to you.
  • WhatsApp sharing: When you tap share, the app hands a text summary to the app you select. We do not see or store it.
  • Children: The app is not directed at children under 13.
  • Deleting data: Uninstalling the app or using the in-app reset removes all data.
