FULL DESCRIPTION:
Keep all your money in one place with YaarKhata.

* Multiple accounts - bank accounts and cash, each with its own balance
* Expenses, income and adjustments - balances update automatically
* Reports - see spending by category and month
* Friend ledger - track who owes you and what you owe, kept separate from your expenses
* Partial payments and "settle all" for any person
* Share a debt summary on WhatsApp
* Backup and restore your data with a JSON file
* No ads, no sign-up, no internet needed - your data stays on your phone
